## Design Context

### Users
- **Customers**: People looking for restaurants, wanting to join digital queues or make reservations. They're typically on mobile phones, often hungry and in a hurry. They want speed, clarity, and confidence that their spot is secured.
- **Restaurant staff/admins**: Restaurant managers and hosts managing queues, tables, and reservations in real-time. They work on tablets or desktops in fast-paced environments.

### Brand Personality
**Bold, Refined, Practical**

- **Bold**: Confident red accents, strong typography, decisive interactions
- **Refined**: Warm neutrals (brown, beige), sophisticated but not stuffy, quality feel
- **Practical**: Fast, no-nonsense, every element earns its place

Emotional goals: confidence, hunger/appetite, trust, efficiency.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone**: Warm sophistication — like a well-designed restaurant menu or a premium food app
- **Color palette**: Red (energy, appetite, CTAs), Brown/Beige (comfort, warmth, surfaces), Black (contrast, premium feel). Purple is explicitly avoided (negative food association).
- **Theme**: Both light and dark, with toggle. Light for daytime browsing, dark for evening/ambient contexts.
- **Anti-references**: Generic AI slop (cyan/purple gradients, glassmorphism), basic templates without identity, cold corporate ERP systems.

### Design Principles
1. **Appetite-first**: Every visual choice should subconsciously evoke warmth and appetite — warm tones, inviting spacing, food-context photography.
2. **Speed over spectacle**: Animations serve clarity (state changes, feedback), never decoration. Users are hungry and impatient.
3. **Hierarchy through weight**: Use size and weight contrasts to guide the eye, not color proliferation. The red accent works because it's rare.
4. **Context-adaptive**: Mobile customers at the restaurant door need different layouts than desktop admins managing tables. Design for context, not just viewport.
5. **Tactile confidence**: Every interactive element should feel intentional — snappy transitions, clear feedback, no ambiguity about what's tappable.
